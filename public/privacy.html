Consent and age

We create an account only after you tick the boxes to accept this notice and confirm you are 13 or older (or a parent or guardian agrees for you). You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Your rights

From Account in the game you can download everything we hold about you, correct your nickname, sign out of all devices, or permanently delete your account. Deleting removes your account, WP and items, and cannot be undone. You can also write to our Data Protection Officer to ask for access, correction or withdrawal of consent.

Who else handles data (transfers outside Singapore)

Google or Apple verifies your identity when you sign in. Our host (Vercel) and database (Upstash Redis) store the data above and may be located outside Singapore. We use them under terms that protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA. Optional realtime delivery is provided by Ably, which only sees room codes and version numbers. Chat messages may be sent to MyMemory (translated.net) for translation, as described above; this happens only in the browser of the person reading the message, and only when their browser cannot translate by itself.

Protection and retention

Provider IDs are hashed with a secret key; sessions are random 256-bit codes stored only as hashes and expire after 14 idle days or 30 days; sign-in codes can be used once; WP earned from play is capped per day. Accounts inactive for 12 months are scheduled for deletion. If a breach is likely to cause significant harm we will notify affected people and the PDPC as the law requires.

Guests

Guests can play and earn WP inside a room. Guest WP and purchases are kept only in that room and disappear when the guest leaves or the room expires.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will ask you again before continuing to use your data.
